NFPA 72-2022 CHAPTER 17 — INITIATING DEVICES (CONTINUED)
Section 17.6 Heat Detectors

17.6.1 General.
Heat detectors shall be installed in all areas where required by other NFPA standards, codes, federal, state, and local laws.

17.6.2 Types of Heat Detectors.
17.6.2.1 Fixed-Temperature Detectors.
Fixed-temperature detectors actuate when the detecting element reaches a specified temperature. Common ratings include:
- 57°C (135°F): Standard — for general areas where ambient temperature does not exceed 38°C (100°F).
- 79°C (174°F): High — for areas where ambient temperature is 38°C to 66°C (100°F to 150°F).
- 121°C (250°F): Extra-High — for areas where ambient temperature is 66°C to 107°C (150°F to 225°F).

17.6.2.2 Rate-of-Rise (ROR) Detectors.
Rate-of-rise detectors actuate when the temperature rises at a rate exceeding 8.3°C per minute (15°F per minute), regardless of the actual ambient temperature. ROR detectors should not be used in areas where rapid temperature increases occur normally (e.g., manufacturing, cooking areas).

17.6.2.3 Combination Fixed-Temperature / Rate-of-Rise Detectors.
These devices respond to either condition first.

17.6.3 Spacing of Heat Detectors.
17.6.3.1 Listed Spacing.
The listed spacing for heat detectors is based on a smooth ceiling at a height of 3.05 m (10 ft) under standard conditions. The most common listed spacing is 9.1 m × 9.1 m (30 ft × 30 ft) = 83.6 m² (900 ft²) per detector.

17.6.3.2 Ceiling Height Corrections.
The listed spacing shall be reduced per Table 17.6.3.2 when ceiling heights exceed 3.05 m (10 ft):

| Ceiling Height | Correction Factor |
|---------------|-------------------|
| Up to 3.05 m (10 ft)   | 1.00 (no reduction) |
| 3.05 – 3.66 m (10–12 ft) | 0.91 |
| 3.66 – 4.57 m (12–15 ft) | 0.84 |
| 4.57 – 6.10 m (15–20 ft) | 0.75 |
| 6.10 – 7.62 m (20–25 ft) | 0.69 |
| 7.62 – 9.14 m (25–30 ft) | 0.59 |

17.6.3.3 Beam Ceilings.
Where the ceiling consists of solid beams more than 100 mm (4 in.) deep and spaced less than 9.1 m (30 ft) apart, detectors shall be installed in each beam bay.

17.6.3.4 Sloped Ceilings.
17.6.3.4.1 For sloped ceilings with a slope greater than 1 in 8, a row of detectors shall be installed at the high side of the area within 900 mm (36 in.) of the ridge.
17.6.3.4.2 Additional rows shall be spaced at not more than the listed spacing measured horizontally.

17.6.4 Ceiling Height Limitations for Heat Detectors.
17.6.4.1 Listed spot-type heat detectors shall not be used on ceilings higher than 9.14 m (30 ft) unless the detector is listed and tested for higher installation heights.
17.6.4.2 In areas with ceilings exceeding 9.14 m, alternative detection strategies (e.g., linear heat detection cable, aspirating systems) shall be considered.

17.6.5 Linear Heat Detection.
17.6.5.1 Linear heat detection cable shall be listed for the purpose.
17.6.5.2 The cable shall be installed and spaced per the manufacturer's instructions.
17.6.5.3 Typical spacing: parallel runs no more than 9.1 m (30 ft) apart for standard applications, reduced for high-hazard areas.

HEAT DETECTOR SELECTION GUIDE (BAZspark occupancy mapping):
- Kitchen / Boiler Room: Fixed-temperature 79°C or higher; NOT rate-of-rise due to normal rapid temperature changes.
- General Offices: Rate-of-rise + Fixed combination (57°C) preferred.
- Parking Garages: Fixed-temperature 57°C or 79°C; rate-of-rise acceptable if heating is not rapid.
- Electrical Rooms: Fixed-temperature 57°C; ROR acceptable.
- Attics: Fixed-temperature 57°C at ridge level; linear heat cable alternative.

AMBIENT TEMPERATURE MARGINS:
- The detector rating must exceed the maximum expected ambient temperature by at least 19°C (35°F) to prevent false alarms.
- Example: If the ambient peak is 50°C (122°F), minimum detector rating = 50 + 19 = 69°C → use 79°C rated detector.

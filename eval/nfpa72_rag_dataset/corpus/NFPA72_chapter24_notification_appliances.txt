NFPA 72-2022 CHAPTER 24 — NOTIFICATION APPLIANCES
Section 24.4 Audible Characteristics

24.4.1 General.
24.4.1.1 Audible notification appliances shall produce a sound level not less than 15 dB above the average ambient sound level or 5 dB above the maximum sound level having a duration of at least 60 seconds, whichever is greater, measured 1.5 m (5 ft) above the floor in the area served by the appliance.

24.4.1.2 The sound level shall not exceed 110 dB(A) at the minimum hearing distance from the device.

24.4.2 Public Operating Mode.
24.4.2.1 Audible appliances shall have a sound level of not less than 65 dB(A) at 3 m (10 ft) in public areas.
24.4.2.2 Where the occupancy is used for sleeping, the minimum sound level shall be 75 dB(A) at the pillow level for sleeping areas.

24.4.3 Private Operating Mode.
Audible appliances in private operating mode shall have a sound level of not less than 45 dB(A) at 3 m (10 ft) or 10 dB(A) above the ambient sound level, whichever is greater.

24.4.4 Maximum Sound Pressure.
24.4.4.1 The maximum SPL for audible appliances shall not exceed 120 dB peak sound pressure.
24.4.4.2 Appliances producing a sound level in excess of 100 dB(A) shall not be installed within 1 m (3 ft) of any occupant's normal head position.

24.5 Visible Characteristics

24.5.1 General.
24.5.1.1 Visible notification appliances (strobes) shall be installed in all areas where audible appliances are required and shall meet or exceed the requirements of this section.

24.5.2.1 The flash rate for single strobes shall be 1 Hz minimum to 2 Hz maximum (1 to 2 flashes per second). For rooms with two or more devices, the devices shall be synchronized.

24.5.2.4 Candela (cd) Requirements for Rooms.
The required cd rating for rooms based on room area is specified in Table 18.5.4.3.5 of NFPA 72-2022:
- 9.3 m² to 27.9 m² (100 ft² to 300 ft²): 15 cd minimum.
- 27.9 m² to 46.5 m² (300 ft² to 500 ft²): 30 cd minimum.
- 46.5 m² to 93.0 m² (500 ft² to 1000 ft²): 75 cd minimum.
- 93.0 m² to 186.0 m² (1000 ft² to 2000 ft²): 110 cd minimum.
- 186.0 m² to 278.5 m² (2000 ft² to 3000 ft²): 135 cd minimum.
- 278.5 m² to 371.6 m² (3000 ft² to 4000 ft²): 185 cd minimum.

24.5.3 Spacing.
24.5.3.1 Corridor Spacing:
Visible appliances shall be installed so that no point in any corridor is more than 15.25 m (50 ft) from the visible appliance.
Where the corridor is greater than 15.25 m (50 ft) long, the devices shall be spaced no more than 30.5 m (100 ft) apart.

24.5.3.2 Room Spacing (single device):
The strobe shall be installed in the upper portion of a room, with the lens 2.03 m to 2.44 m (80 to 96 in.) above the finished floor.
The maximum coverage area per device depends on cd rating per Table 18.5.4.3.5.

24.5.5 Sleeping Areas.
24.5.5.1 Visible appliances in sleeping areas shall have a minimum cd intensity of 177 cd or 110 cd with an oblong pattern.
24.5.5.2 Visible appliances in sleeping areas shall be located within 1.83 m (6 ft) of the pillow centerline.

NOTIFICATION APPLIANCE CIRCUIT (NAC) REQUIREMENTS:
- NAC voltage: Typically 12V or 24V DC (nominal).
- End-of-Line (EOL) resistance: Required on each circuit for supervision.
- Maximum current: Limited by FACP panel rating per circuit (commonly 1.0 A to 3.0 A per NAC).
- Wire sizing: Shall be based on voltage drop calculations such that voltage at the last device is within device operating range.

VOLTAGE DROP FORMULA FOR NAC:
  V_drop = (2 × L × I × R_per_meter)
  Where:
    L = one-way wire length in meters
    I = total current draw (amperes)
    R_per_meter = wire resistance per meter (Ω/m) — 14 AWG = 0.00843 Ω/m; 12 AWG = 0.00529 Ω/m

MINIMUM TERMINAL VOLTAGE:
  Device terminal voltage = V_supply - V_drop ≥ V_device_min (typically 20.4 V for 24 V devices)
